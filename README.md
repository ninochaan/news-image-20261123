A newspaper front page that sums up Monday 23 November 2026 in a single
image — 44 stories: Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO and shares soar
31%, Zelenskyy says Ukrainian sea drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury
off Libya, Iran vows "all means" against the US blockade, the Dymer toll
climbs as a boy of 7 is pulled alive from the rubble, COP31's failure
registers the morning after, NATO vows to defend "every inch" in Berlin,
the EU proposes a 22nd sanctions package on Russia, Houthis claim a Rabigh
refinery hit, the Banten candy factory fire kills 24 with 34 missing, a
national strike shuts Belgian airports, Bitcoin spikes to $64,500 then fades
to $61,100, Democrats reach 258 House seats, Tyson Fury reveals his
schizophrenia diagnosis, BepiColombo's Mercury orbit entry needs software
checks, a M6.5 quake strikes 598 km under the Vanuatu region, and India's
720/9 ends in a Wellington draw — plus 28 numbered one-line briefs, a "WHERE
IT HAPPENED" world map, a big-numbers stat strip, and a DATE CHECK box
naming the big stories left out because they happened on another day.

Every headline is a verified 23 November 2026 event (sources in the build
workspace's STORY-POOL.md); one-party claims stay attributed. The image is
drawn deterministically by `gen.py` (Pillow, no network, no timestamps, no
randomness) and written to `entry.png` (1024×1024). `main.ts` copies
`entry.png` into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.
